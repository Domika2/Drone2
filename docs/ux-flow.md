# Trendix UX Flow & Information Architecture

## Primary journeys
### Customers
1. Land on **Home** → choose a use-case story tile.
2. Enter **Explore** → filter by delivery speed, vendor rating, locality, and price.
3. View **Story-first product page** → read origin story, shipping lane, and trust signals.
4. Add to **Storyline cart** → see dropshipper summary and vendor confirmations.
5. **Checkout** with multi-gateway payments.
6. **Track order** through real-time status and timeline updates.

### Vendors
1. **Onboard** with identity verification and product authenticity checks.
2. **Vendor hub** dashboard shows inventory, orders, pricing control, and analytics.
3. **Dropshipper access** toggle at product or collection level.
4. **Fulfillment** updates sync automatically to customer-facing timelines.

### Dropshippers
1. **Approval** via vendor connection requests.
2. **Import** products into curated storefronts.
3. **Profit controls** with margin rules and dynamic pricing.
4. **Auto-order routing** to vendors + fulfillment sync.

## Page map
- Home (story-based, value-driven)
- Explore (problem-solution discovery)
- Vendor Hubs
- Dropshipper Hubs
- Product Story Pages
- Cart & Checkout
- User Dashboards (role-based)
- Help & Resolution Center

## Navigation logic (non-grid)
- Story map navigation: use-case tiles lead to panels.
- Collection navigator: vertical timeline of curated sets.
- Search results are grouped by story clusters, not by category grids.
